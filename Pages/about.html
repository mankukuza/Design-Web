About

our Visual Savvy

"Visual Savvy” believes that culture provides humanity with a sense of identity, telling us who we are, where we came from, and where we belong in the world, yet many cultures, especially performing arts, are fading from history. We utilize motion capture and mixed reality technologies to preserve the grace of these cultural expressions while curating vibrant digital assets."

Co-Founder & Product Designer He/Him

Sarach Kunapermsiri

in @ Your Social Link

Sarach is leading in product design specializing in furniture and home decor. He has keen senses of how objects relate to space. He has been working at the award-winning design studio Atelier2+ as a product designer. His work has been showcased at notable exhibitions like Bangkok Design Week and Style Fair. Sarach earned a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Design with first-class honors from King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang, focusing on furniture design. He is skilled in both industrial and craftsmanship processes, combining aesthetics and storytelling to giving life into spaces where his designs are placed.
